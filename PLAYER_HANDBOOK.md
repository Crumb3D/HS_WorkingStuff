# HS Working Stuff — Player Handbook

**Make a house a home**

Flip a POI into a place you want to live in. Use the loos and taps you find while you’re out on jobs. Craft the white goods and the consumer unit later, when you’re ready.

A printable copy is `Handbook.html` in this mod folder (open in a browser, then Print / save as PDF).

Works on 7 Days To Die **3.2 and 3.3**. Load this mod on the dedicated server and every client.

---

## The plan

You do not have to craft a whole kitchen to move in. Find a house you like, clear it, and **repair what’s already there**. Cupboards become empty storage for your stuff. The cooker, toaster, fridge, TVs, boiler and radiators become working versions of the same things.

When you want UK sockets, a consumer unit, a white fridge-freezer or a proper washer, **craft those and upgrade the house**. Until then, live in the POI.

Nailgun, claw hammer or stone axe, **right click**. Look for the upgrade hits, not a red X. If you see an X, you’re missing the parts or that block has nothing to upgrade (chairs are already intact — pick them up).

## Day one in a house

1. Loot the toilets and the gore bath. An empty POI toilet becomes usable. Urinals already work. Empty tubs and clawfoot baths work as soon as you find them (they’ll tell you they need pipes and a boiler for a proper wash).
2. Empty the fridge, cooker, microwave, coffee maker, toaster, washer or dryer, then repair them with **2 electric parts**.
3. Shut open cabinets, desks, nightstands, lockers and chests with **wood** (scrap iron for metal bathroom cabinets). They repair to **empty** cupboards you can fill yourself — not a fresh unsearched loot ping.
4. Repair the wall TV and the house radiator the same way. Wire the TV from a socket when you have one. The POI cylinder water heater repairs into a working boiler.
5. Pee at the toilet, a urinal, or a tree. Wash at a sink (1 jar) or a lake. You can live here before you own a single crafted appliance.

## What you can repair

| What you find | Parts | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open / broken kitchen cabinets, wall cupboards, desks, nightstands, armoires, bookcases, chests, closets, lockers, file cabinets | Wood (or scrap iron for metal) | Closed **empty** storage |
| Medicine cabinet | Scrap iron | Closed empty cabinet |
| Fridge, cooker, wall oven, microwave, coffee maker, toaster, washer, dryer | 2 electric parts | Working appliance (same look) |
| POI TVs (stand and wall, including the big 2×2) | 2 electric parts | Working TV, same size. Wire it from a socket |
| House radiator | Forged iron | Working cast-iron radiator. Pipe it to a boiler |
| POI water heater | Forged iron | Working boiler (vanilla tank) |
| POI toilet (loot it) / gore bath (loot it) | — | Working toilet / empty tub |

Empty the loot first on kitchen appliances. Inside a land claim you can pick the working ones up later if you want to rearrange.

## Out on jobs

You will not always be home when you need a pee or a wash. That’s fine.

- **Toilet / urinal** in any POI: loot the toilet once, then Hold E to pee (and poo on a toilet). A dry toilet takes about three wees before it needs a jar to flush. A poo always needs that jar, or a pipe.
- **Sink** if it’s plumbed, or any sink you can use to wash up with a jar.
- **Lake or river:** crouch and use the drink key to wash. Standing in water up to your waist washes more off slowly, and rinses a bit of dirt off your clothes.
- **Tree:** look at the trunk, Hold E, have a pee. Not for a poo.

You do not need a land claim just to *use* a POI loo. Claim is for picking it up and taking it home.

## Later: white goods and breakers

When the magazines and the perk catch up, craft the kit that makes the house yours instead of “a repaired POI”.

### Power

- UK sockets (single / double) or US outlets. Wire from a generator into a socket, then from the socket into the appliance. You see the plug in the outlet.
- **Consumer unit (UK)** or **breaker panel (US)** when you want proper circuits. A circuit is dead until it has a fuse or breaker. Name the circuits. The main switch kills the lot.
- Fuses are cheap and blow. Breakers cost more and you reset them. 5A / 15A / 30A / 45A (about 5W per amp).

### White goods

- UK fridge-freezer (tall white), chest freezer, UK washer and dryer — or keep using the repaired US vanilla ones in the kitchen.
- Combi boiler on the wall, panel or cast-iron radiators, wall thermostat. Pipe cold from a tank, heating under the floor.
- Shower and bath once hot water is piped. Crafted toilets and urinals if you want them in a new build.

You can mix: live with the repaired POI cooker and toaster for weeks, then hang a consumer unit and swap in a white fridge-freezer when you can unlock it.

## Heat, wash, food, telly

- **Temperature.** A house only knocks a bit off the weather. Radiators heat a room up to about 20°C / 68°F. Air con cools to about 22°C / 72°F. Look at a wall thermometer.
- **Smell and clothes.** You get unwashed after a day. Clothes get dirty over about three days. Washer needs power and a cold pipe; clothes come out wet. Dryer, washing line or airer to dry them.
- **Bladder and bowels.** Idle fill is slow (about a day / three days of game time). Drinking and eating speed it up. Don’t wait until 100% unless you like accidents.
- **Food.** Food spoils in about 7 days. A powered fridge slows it; a freezer stops it (and freezes it solid). Wheat → flour → dough → loaf → slices → toaster.
- **Cuppa.** Plug the kettle in. Mug on the worktop, tea bag in, pour. Murky water is fine, the kettle boils it.
- **TV / hi-fi.** Wire from a socket. Put your own `.mp4` files in `%AppData%\Roaming\7DaysToDie\HSWorkingStuff\TV` and music in the `Music` folder. Everyone in multiplayer needs the same files on their PC.

## What you have to learn

Crafted recipes need **both** a magazine level and a perk. Repairing a POI fridge does not. The list in the crafting menu tells you which magazine and perk each recipe wants.

| Magazine | Level | Perk | You can craft |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Electrician | 25 | Advanced Engineering 1 | Microwaves, coffee, kettle, toasters, TVs, hi-fi, cookers, sockets, switches, consumer unit / breaker panel, fuses |
| Electrician | 45 | Advanced Engineering 2 | Fridge, fridge-freezer, chest freezer, combi, air con, breakers |
| Workstations | 16 | Advanced Engineering 1 | Pipe tool, pipes, tank, sink, rain catch, thermometer, washing line, airer |
| Workstations | 26 | Advanced Engineering 2 | Radiators, thermostat, shower, bath, washers, dryers, toilet, urinal |
| Home Cooking | 6–26 | Master Chef | Drying rack, cooler, bread, mugs, grills |
| Seeds | 10 / 18 | Living Off The Land 1 | Compost, wheat |

Microwave and grill food still uses the vanilla cooking unlocks. If you can’t cook it on a campfire, you can’t microwave it either.

## Quick tips

- Pipe tool: Hold E. **New water system** on a tank or rain catch, **New connection** on the next thing. Pipe never crosses open air. Sinks feed from below; radiators under the floor.
- A socket only takes as many plugs as it has outlets. Chain sockets along the wall without using a plug.
- Appliances only draw watts while they work. An idle cooker costs nothing until you queue food.
- God mode clears the mod’s smell, dirty/wet clothes, and needing the toilet.
- Load this mod on the **dedicated server and every client**. After an update, restart both.
