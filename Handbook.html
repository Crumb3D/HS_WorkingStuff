Make a house a home

Flip a POI into a place you want to live in. Use the loos and taps you find while you’re out on jobs. Craft the white goods and the consumer unit later, when you’re ready.

The plan

You do not have to craft a whole kitchen to move in. Find a house you like, clear it, and repair what’s already there. Cupboards become empty storage for your stuff. The cooker, toaster, fridge, TVs, boiler and radiators become working versions of the same things.

When you want UK sockets, a consumer unit, a white fridge-freezer or a proper washer, craft those and upgrade the house. Until then, live in the POI.

Nailgun, claw hammer or stone axe, right click. Look for the upgrade hits, not a red X. If you see an X, you’re missing the parts or that block has nothing to upgrade (chairs are already intact — pick them up).

Day one in a house

  1. Loot the toilets and the gore bath. An empty POI toilet becomes usable. Urinals already work. Empty tubs, clawfoot baths and shower heads work as soon as you find them (they’ll tell you they need pipes and a boiler for a proper wash). Empty jars often come out of the loo, sinks and cupboards — keep them for flushing.
  2. Empty the fridge, cooker, microwave, coffee maker, toaster, washer or dryer, then repair them with 2 electric parts.
  3. Shut open cabinets, desks, nightstands, lockers and chests with wood (scrap iron for metal bathroom cabinets). They repair to empty cupboards you can fill yourself — not a fresh unsearched loot ping.
  4. Repair the wall TV and the house radiator the same way. Wire the TV from a socket when you have one. The POI cylinder water heater repairs into a working boiler.
  5. Pee at the toilet, a urinal, or a tree. Wash at a sink (1 jar) or a lake. You can live here before you own a single crafted appliance.

Out on jobs

You will not always be home when you need a pee or a wash. That’s fine.

  • Toilet / urinal in any POI: loot the toilet once, then Hold E to pee (and poo on a toilet). A dry toilet takes about three wees before it needs a jar to flush. A poo always needs that jar, or a pipe. Empty jars turn up in loos, sinks and cupboards (often in the toilet loot itself). Murky lake water is fine.
  • Sink if it’s plumbed, or any sink you can use to wash up with a jar.
  • Shower / bath in a POI: the head and the empty tub already work. They still need pipes and a boiler for a proper wash.
  • Lake or river: crouch and use the drink key to wash. Standing in water up to your waist washes more off slowly, and rinses a bit of dirt off your clothes.
  • Tree: look at the trunk, Hold E, have a pee. Not for a poo.

You do not need a land claim just to use a POI loo. Claim is for picking it up and taking it home.

A few days: pipes

Workstation magazine 6 and Advanced Engineering rank 1 unlock the pipe tool, pipes, intake, tank, sink, rain catch, toilet, urinal, shower and bath. They are hand-crafted. You do not need a workbench. A few days of looting is enough to pipe a toilet and a shower.

A toilet works from the tank, or from a jar poured into the cistern. It does not need power. The shower and bath are in the same unlock, and a proper hot wash still waits on a powered boiler.

The water cooler, thermometer and washing line stay at magazine 16. Radiators, the washer and the dryer stay at 26, because heating and the machines wait on power.

Later: white goods and breakers

When the electrician magazines and the perk catch up, craft the kit that makes the house yours instead of “a repaired POI”.

Power

  • UK sockets (single / double) or US outlets. They only go on a solid wall. Wire from a generator into a socket, then from the socket into the appliance. You see the plug in the outlet. The lead stays on a wall and goes around windows. Put the socket on the wall first, then place a kettle, toaster or hi-fi in that same block: the socket stays on the wall behind it.
  • Consumer unit (UK) or breaker panel (US) when you want proper circuits. A circuit is dead until it has a breaker. UK switches stand vertical. US switches lie horizontal. Name the circuits. The main switch kills the lot.
  • Breakers are 5A / 15A / 30A / 45A (about 5W per amp). Too much load trips one. Reset it at the panel. They unlock with the consumer unit.

White goods

  • UK fridge-freezer (tall white), chest freezer, UK washer and dryer — or keep using the repaired US vanilla ones in the kitchen.
  • Combi boiler on the wall, or repair the POI cylinder water heater. It is the same boiler: hot tap and central heating, 15W. Panel or cast-iron radiators and the wall thermostat come with workstation magazine 26. Pipe cold from a tank, heating under the floor.

You can mix: live with the repaired POI cooker and toaster for weeks, then hang a consumer unit and swap in a white fridge-freezer when you can unlock it.

Heat, wash, food, telly

  • Temperature. The room keeps the biome it is built in. Brick, concrete and cobble hold desert heat, so that house wants air con, and they hold snow cold, so that house wants radiators (a heavy room wants more than one). Solid steel holds it too, and lets it go sooner than brick: a steel desert house still wants air con, and a steel snow house still wants radiators. Timber lets the weather go sooner, and one unit is usually enough. Corrugated sheet follows the weather. A warm radiator heats the blocks it touches first, then that warmth walks out through the walls. The air in the room follows those blocks. Once the warmth reaches an outside wall it draws zombies, less than a campfire and less than the noise of a running machine. The walls keep the heat or the cold for hours after you switch off. Radiators settle a room near 20°C / 68°F. Air con settles it near 22°C / 72°F. Look at a wall thermometer.
  • Smell and clothes. After about a day you get Unwashed. The icon is how many extra metres zombies can smell you. Running or being hot sweats you, and that builds twice as fast. A hot shower or bath washes it off. Cold water still washes, and it takes about half the smell off for half as long. A sink or a crouch at the water’s edge takes a bit off, once an hour, and a cold sink takes less than a hot one. Clothes you wear get dirty on their own, faster when you are sweaty, and faster again when you walk through fields, crops, mud or sand. At about 20% dirty the Dirty Clothes icon comes on and shows the percent. At about half dirty they start drawing zombies too. Hover a piece to see it. Wash them in the machine. They come out wet. Dryer, washing line or airer to dry them. Wet clothes are not something you craft. An accident does both at once: a pee soaks and dirties what you are wearing, a poo makes it filthy, and you stink straight away.
  • Bladder and bowels. Idle fill is slow (about a day / three days of game time). Drinking and eating speed it up. Don’t wait until 100% unless you like accidents.
  • Food. Food spoils in about 3 days on you, about 7 days in a powered fridge, about 30 days in the fridge-freezer’s freezer and about 90 days in a chest freezer. Looking at the freezer shows those days. Frozen food is named Frozen. It defrosts in 24 hours in your pack or on a chopping board, and in 12 hours in the fridge. The spoil clock starts once it is soft. A chest freezer keeps it frozen. You can also defrost at a microwave, cooker, campfire or grill. The chopping board sits on a counter. Hold E to put food on the board or the drying rack. A tool breaks either of them. Wheat → flour → dough → loaf → slices → toaster.
  • Noise. A running machine puts a Noisy icon up with the smell icons. The number is how many metres zombies can hear it. Walls cut that. Shut the door or switch it off.
  • Cuppa. Plug the kettle in. Mug on the worktop, tea bag in, pour. Murky water is fine, the kettle boils it.
  • TV / hi-fi. Wire from a socket. Put your own .mp4 files in %AppData%\Roaming\7DaysToDie\HSWorkingStuff\TV and music in the Music folder. Everyone in multiplayer needs the same files on their PC.

The house is a dinner bell

You can live here. You cannot run the whole kitchen like a nightclub and expect the street to stay empty.

  • A running washer, dryer, cooker, microwave, toaster, kettle, coffee maker, air con, firing boiler, TV or hi-fi makes noise in the background while you sort loot. Idle zombies hear it and come to the house. The fridge stays quiet.
  • Each side of the room muffles on its own. Brick cuts the most, a closed window less, a shut door some. Open the door and that side lets the washer, the hi-fi and the telly out. The other walls still muffle their side.
  • Smell works the same way. An accident, dirty clothes and carried meat use the vanilla smell. A wall on the way muffles that side the most, a closed window less, a shut door some. Open the door and that side lets it out. Load HS Better Ears.
  • Windows leak more than a solid wall. Repairing and crafting are loud too: crouch if you want to sneak (load HS Better Ears on the server and every client).

What you have to learn

Crafted recipes need both a magazine level and a perk. Repairing a POI fridge does not. Pipes are the early one: workstation magazine 6 and Advanced Engineering 1, and you craft them in your hands. Heating, the washer and the white goods wait on the higher levels below. The crafting menu lists the magazine and perk for each recipe.

Quick tips

  • Pipe tool: Hold E. New water system on a tank or rain catch, New connection on the next thing. There is no length cap. Pipe runs inside a solid floor or along a wall. If it cannot, the tool names the block or the open air at those coordinates. A straight run uses straight pipe, a bend uses a corner, and a branch uses a joint. Sinks feed from below; radiators under the floor. If a pipe already runs through the floor under a sink, toilet or shower, Lay pipes tees a joint up into it and gives the straight piece back.
  • On your land claim, hold E on a coffee maker, cooker, toaster or kettle and Pick up once it is empty. Pouring a stack of jars into a tank adds every jar.
  • When a magazine or a perk unlocks one of these recipes, the game says New Recipe Unlocked, the same line as a schematic.
  • A socket only takes as many plugs as it has outlets. Chain sockets along the wall without using a plug.
  • Appliances only draw watts while they work. An idle cooker costs nothing until you queue food.
  • God mode clears the mod’s smell, dirty/wet clothes, and needing the toilet.
  • Shut the doors if the washer is on. An open door next to a running machine is a dinner bell.
  • Load this mod on the dedicated server and every client. After an update, restart both.
